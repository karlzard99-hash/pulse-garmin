# Coaching rules

Claude checks every plan against these before the user sees it. A plan that breaks one is redone.
**the user can always override a rule.** An override is written to `memory.md` so it sticks.

## Load
- Weekly training time goes up by 10% at most.
- After 3 weeks of rising load in a row, the next week is easy, with about 30% less volume. Flat load never triggers it.
- No more than 2 hard sessions a week, never on back-to-back days.

## Intensity (depends on the goal)
- General health and base: about 80% of the time is easy (you can talk), 20% hard.
- FTP or threshold goal: more "comfortably hard" work, roughly 70/20/10 (easy / threshold / hard).
- VO2 max goal: short blocks with 2 sessions a week of hard intervals.
- Race build: the last 2–3 weeks are close to race pace, then a taper that cuts volume and keeps some intensity.
- Every week has at least 1 full rest day.

## Goals
- Any goal works through a goal profile: its key sessions, its intensity mix, and the test that proves progress
  (FTP test, VO2 max reading, threshold run).

## Recovery signals
- HRV below the normal range 2 days in a row: the next hard session becomes easy.
- Sleep score under 60, or resting HR 5+ above normal: no hard session that day.
- Body battery under 25 in the morning: it is an easy day or a rest day.

## Adapting
- A missed session is dropped, not stacked onto later days.
- 2+ missed sessions in a week: the rest of the week is redrawn.
- Building back after a break of 7+ days starts at 50% of the old volume.

## Default for general health
- 4 days a week, a mix of cardio, strength and mobility, using the sports already done (run, swim, bike).

## Multi-sport days
- A brick (bike then run) or any combination is one session with legs, and goes to the watch as one multi-sport workout.
- A brick counts as one hard session if either leg is hard.
